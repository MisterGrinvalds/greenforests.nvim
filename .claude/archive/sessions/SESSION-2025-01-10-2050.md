### 1. Buffer Navigation
**Decision:** Use Alt+number (1-9) instead of other modifier combos
**Rationale:**

### 2. NeoTree Space Mapping
**Decision:** Set to `'none'` instead of custom workaround
**Rationale:**
- Previous workaround (switching windows) was clunky
- Setting to `'none'` lets global leader keymaps pass through
- Simpler, more maintainable solution
